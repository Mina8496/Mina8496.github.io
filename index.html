About me

I'm a Flutter developer who focuses on scalable, production-ready mobile apps. I structure projects with Clean Architecture and Bloc/Cubit (or Riverpod and GetX when the project calls for it), connect them to REST APIs and Firebase, and build small Node.js and Express backends when an app needs one.

Recent work includes AI-powered features such as plant disease analysis from photos, payment flows with Stripe, PayPal and Paymob, and apps published on Google Play.

Outside of work: reading, coding and music.

Client project, Oct 2026 to present

MyFarm: AI plant health and farming assistant

A Flutter app that identifies plants and analyzes their health from a photo.

  • Analyzes plant images with the Plant.id v3 REST API and shows disease results, confidence scores and localized care information.
  • Built on Clean Architecture with repositories and use cases. Bloc/Cubit and GetX manage state and async flows.
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore handle user data and collaborative group notes.
  • Includes Arabic disease information, text-to-speech, weather updates, cached data and task management.
